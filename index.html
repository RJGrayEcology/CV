Wildlife Data Scientist & Conservation Strategist

I am currently Head of Data for the Global Monitoring System under ECOSOLVE / GI-TOC, coordinating expert analysts from 11 countries and their national, regional, and international law enforcement partners to detect and disrupt illegal wildlife trade networks. My work spans field ecology, machine learning, geospatial intelligence, OSINT, grant management, and AI-powered conservation tools.
