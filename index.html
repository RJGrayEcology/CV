Global Monitoring System
ECOSOLVE · GI-TOC · 2024–Present

As Head of Data for the Global Monitoring System, I direct an 11-country network of OSINT analysts conducting covert digital surveillance of illegal wildlife trade (IWT) networks. Analysts monitor dark web marketplaces, encrypted messaging platforms, social media, and e-commerce ecosystems to develop actionable intelligence packages delivered to national and international law enforcement for ground operations — bridging open-source intelligence with kinetic disruption of transnational organised crime.

Intelligence Capabilities
OSINT Collection & Analysis
End-to-end collection cycles targeting online IWT marketplaces, encrypted channels (Telegram, WhatsApp, Signal), dark-web forums, and social media platforms. Analysts produce structured intelligence reports on trafficking actors, commodities, routes, and pricing.
GeoINT & Pattern-of-Life Analysis
Geospatial intelligence integrating trafficking route data, seizure records, and digital footprints. Produces pattern-of-life assessments of priority suspects and identifies logistical nodes within smuggling networks for law enforcement targeting.
Network Mapping & Link Analysis
Social network analysis and actor mapping of IWT syndicates, identifying key brokers, financiers, and logistics operators within transnational organised crime structures. Supports strategic prioritisation of high-value targets.
AI-Powered Monitoring Tools
Development and deployment of AI/ML pipelines for automated detection of wildlife trade listings across platforms. Supports analysts with classification models, NLP-based keyword systems, and image recognition to scale monitoring coverage.
Law Enforcement Coordination
Direct liaison with national wildlife crime units, INTERPOL, UNODC, and regional task forces. Intelligence packages formatted as actionable case files delivered to partners for arrest operations, customs intercepts, and financial investigations.
Team Leadership & Tradecraft Training
Recruiting, onboarding, and developing analysts across 11 countries. Establishing SOPs for digital security, source handling, intelligence product formatting, and platform-specific collection tradecraft. Maintains operational security across a distributed global team.
